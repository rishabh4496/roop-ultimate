## Read this first

Measurement only: nothing in the render path was changed, and no setting was tuned. The numbers below are the
reference a later stage has to match; this section says how far each can be trusted, and what the probes found
that nobody had asked about. Everything quoted here is generated from `baseline_2026-10-04.json` (+ the
`_pinned_*.json` supplements); the tables further down are those same records.

Machine: RTX 4070 12 GB (the MAIN profile), 31.7 GB RAM, `config.yaml` read live: hyperswap + Restore Ultra +
DFL XSeg, TensorRT/mixed, hevc_nvenc, 10 threads, stabilize face/mask/enhancer on, temporal detection + identity
lock on, `detector_engine: scrfd`. **Not measured: the RTX 3060.**

### 1. Can the reference be reproduced? Only with `ROOP_STAB_CHUNK_MB` pinned.

- **Pinned to the value the first run derived (the `chunk MB` column), every repeat had a bit-identical output video**
  (decoded-video md5) and identical counters and swap audit: d4 three times (A + two pinned), d1 twice, Love twice, d6 twice.
  `rows.csv` was also identical for d4, d1 and Love. **For d6 it was not**: 2 of its 13 columns differ, `d_p0` (the post-render
  grader's embedding distance, 367 of 976 rows, max |diff| 0.094) and `why` (40 rows, an audit sub-tag attributed to person 0 in
  one run and person 1 in the other on the same frame); the video, the audit counts and the per-person verdicts are identical.
  So the md5 is the reference to compare; `rows.csv` is exact on 720p/1080p here but not on the 4K grader.
  The hashes in the summary are real references *if* the later stage exports that `ROOP_STAB_CHUNK_MB`.
- **Unpinned, the same config is not the same render.** d4 run B differs from run A in md5 and rows.csv. The only
  difference found is the stabilizer geometry: at the decision point 6.6 GB of RAM was available in A and 10.2 GB in
  B, giving `10 blocks x 24f` (1 round) vs `20 blocks x 24f` (2 rounds). `faces_seen` (936) and all counters were equal.
  This is the "output depends on free RAM" rule in AGENTS.md, reproduced on the production config.
- **Speed: the first run of a clip was slower than an immediate identical repeat, and the cause is not isolated.**
  d4 10.12 -> 15.00 / 14.48 fps (first run 31% lower than the pinned pair), d1 4.75 -> 6.59 (28% lower), Love 19.63 -> 19.98
  (+1.8%, no penalty), d6 0.29 -> 0.48 (first run 40% lower; `blend` 2.74 s -> 1.58 s per face on identical work). The d4 first run followed a 120-frame same-size warm-up and had the same
  geometry and output as its repeats, so neither geometry nor the output explains it. Rule for later stages:
  discard the first arm, and use the warm repeats as the fps reference. The pinned d4 pair differs by 3.5% (that
  is the usable null: 15.00 vs 14.48 fps).
- The unpinned geometry change (10 vs 20 blocks) itself cost nothing visible: run B (20 blocks) ran 15.01 fps, equal
  to the pinned 10-block repeats (15.00 / 14.48).

### 2. What the probes show

**Sessions (`[Session]`).** Every session in every run bound `TensorrtExecutionProvider` first, with `trt_fp16=on`
(read from the live session, `trt_precision: mixed`); no session came up on another provider and no step-down was flagged:
`swapper:hyperswap` (hyperswap_1a_256.onnx, `source:Nx512,target:Nx3x256x256`), `enhancer:restoreformer++`
(Restore Ultra's network, `1x3x512x512`), `mask:xseg`, and buffalo_l `detection` / `recognition` / `landmark_2d_106` /
`landmark_3d_68`. Of the five construction sites you named, four ran under this config; **`retinaface._build_one`
never ran** (the engine is scrfd), so it is wired and unit-tested but unobserved. Not instrumented: the occluder
mask session (`Mask_Occluder` is initialised in every run) and the CPU-only converter sessions.
The buffalo_l analyser is built **six times** per process (`analyser build #1..#6` lines): #1-#2 with all modules
(before a render sets the module list, which is why `genderage` shows up), #3-#6 with
`detection/landmark_2d_106/landmark_3d_68/recognition` (the render's pool). `genderage` made **0** `get` calls.

**VRAM (`[VRAM]`, device-wide).** d4: 3.4 GB used (nvidia-smi) after `initialize:faceswap`, 3.8 GB with all processors
ready, +665 MiB across the temporal pre-pass, then released again (-490 MiB at "auxiliary sessions for replay"), and
**7.3 GB at frame 500**. Peak GPU memory from the telemetry sampler: 6.9-7.8 GB on every render, with ~1.0-1.5 GB of it
belonging to other applications before the run. torch's `mem_get_info` and nvidia-smi differ by 0.27-0.6 GB at
the same instant (cause not isolated). The every-500-frames sample only exists for runs of 500+ frames (d4, Love);
d1 and d6 have the end-of-run sample.

**Threads (`[Threads]`).** OpenCV runs **1** thread, on the main thread and on the first worker (`cv2.getNumThreads=1`,
`ROOP_RUNTIME_CV_THREADS=1`). torch reports 24 intra-op and 24 inter-op threads; threadpoolctl lists OpenBLAS pools of
24 (numpy's and scipy's) and an OpenMP pool of 24 (libiomp5md) against **10 frame workers**. Mean CPU use was 16-18% of
32 logical threads (peaks 59-100%). Whether those library pools contend was not measured.

**Stabilizer geometry (`[StabGeometry]`, `redundant = WU/block`).**
A block's warm-up is *extra* work in front of its frames, so the share of all processed frames that is thrown away is
WU/(block+WU); both are printed, and the measured counters agree with the formula within the first-block effect.

| clip | frame MB | RAM available at the decision | derived chunk MB | workers x block | WU/block | WU/(block+WU) predicted | measured discarded |

| d1 1080p | 5.93 | 5.7 GB | 859.8 | 10 x 12 | 0.50 | 33.3% | 32.8% |

RAM available at the decision is ~7 GB lower than before the run (14.1 GB -> 6.6 GB on d4); the process holds
8.5-9.6 GB RSS by then.

**Detection work (`BASELINE COUNTERS`).** The counter on the detector model (`det_10g.detect`) equals the counter on the
`_detect_faces_raw` wrapper in every phase of every run, so the numbers below are executions, not intentions.

"Returned" counts faces a rescue handed back when the first pass was short; it does not say they were correct
faces. The ladder fires on 24% of d4 frames (145 of 613 `_detect_faces` calls had an empty first pass) and the
3-turn partial-miss rescue is entered on 66% of them (405 of 613) because `expected_count=2`.
The main pass re-uses the pre-pass faces: its detector executions come from ROI crops and the outcome-guard
re-detect (d4: 170 + 37 in warm-up), and the recognition / 106-point / 68-point models run **only in the pre-pass**
(1271 calls each on d4, 0 in the main pass). Per AGENTS.md the stage share is not a speedup budget, and none of
this was tested as one.

**A rescue that cannot run (and a pyramid that never does).** `pyramid.*` counters are absent in all runs: the
multi-scale detector is only reached by the retinaface engines. Separately, `_detect_faces` computes
`has_multiscale = engine in ('retinaface','retinaface_r50') or bool(detector_scale_pyramid)`, and the config value
`'auto'` is a non-empty string, so on SCRFD the **close-up (320) and padded rescues are skipped whenever the first pass
is empty**: 144 skips on d4 and 165 on Love (`rescue.downscaled.skipped_with_no_pyramid_engine`,
`rescue.padded.skipped_with_no_pyramid_engine`), 0 attempts. Not changed (measurement only); it is a decision for you
whether those two rescues should be live on SCRFD.

**Other facts worth knowing.** 0 frames raised and 0 swallowed detector failures in all ten renders; `scenedetect` is not
installed so `scene_detector` runs its fallback (printed in every run). d6 at 4K spends 48% of thread time in `blend`
(2.74 s per swapped face, against 60 ms on d4), and with 3 workers that is on the order of three quarters of the
loop's wall time; this is a lead, not a measured speedup. The Love window contains the man as well as the woman; the 464
faces refused as `no track entry matched` are, from the capture setup (one faceset, one captured person), most likely him.

### 3. Limits, and decisions that are yours

- **Threads: 10, from `config.yaml` (`max_threads: 10`, `_threads_basis: user`), not the 20 in AGENTS.md.** You said "with my config.yaml"; the
  standing `--threads 20` rule predates the config change. One flag re-renders any of this at 20.
- **Windows were my choice:** d4 0..600 (the repo's established window), Love 1100..1700 (600 frames spanning the kiss onset at
  1296), d1 and d6 whole (418 and 488 frames, **below the 600-frame acceptance minimum**, so treat them as references, not
  acceptance numbers). d1 and d6 use the app's own auto-capture (budget raised to 900 s so the scan finishes; d6 chose seed
  frame 456); d4 and Love use their pinned captures (4930; `--capture 3024 --capture-face 0`).
- The harness fixes `video_quality=12` (config says 14) and the grading pass runs after the render in the same process; neither
  is in the fps numbers.
- A single machine, single day.
