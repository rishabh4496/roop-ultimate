# Auxiliary models in FP16 (xseg, 2d106det, 1k3d68) - 2026-10-11

**Result: NOT shipped. All three stay FP32 (`precision_policy.PRECISION_OVERRIDES`). No strategy of FP32 op exclusions cleared the gates, and the FP16 ONNX is not faster on the CUDA EP for two of the three models.**

Brief: convert the three to FP16 with `onnxconverter_common`, exclude sensitive ops, default to FP16 on compute capability >= 8.0, fall back to FP32 as an override, and confirm no coordinate drift in the 106-point mesh.

## What was built

- `tools/convert_fp16.py` - converts to FP16 weights/compute with FP32 graph I/O (callers unchanged), strategies `all | norm | head | norm_head | bn`. Writes `app/models/fp16/<stem>.<strategy>.onnx` (gitignored; never over the shipped file). Two converter traps handled and asserted:
  1. `onnxconverter_common` default `max_finite_val=1e4` **silently clamps** a 52808.9 constant in 1k3d68 to 10000 (FP16 holds 65504). Now 65504.
  2. With `Gemm` kept FP32 the graph **output came out `float16`** (2d106det, 1k3d68). A cast back to FLOAT is inserted and the tool refuses any non-FLOAT I/O.
- `app/tests/trt_precision_fidelity.py onnx16` - scores each variant on the CUDA EP against the CUDA-FP32 / TF32-off reference, on the same 500 captured real faces (d4, d1, d6, Love, s7) and the same gates as the 10-10 session.

## Why the CUDA EP

TensorRT already builds its own FP16 engine under `mixed`; an FP16 ONNX adds nothing there (and with `trt_fp16` off TRT runs the FP16 nodes in FP32). The path an FP16 ONNX *changes* is the CUDA EP, i.e. the RTX 3060, where TensorRT is not admitted. Note also that a TRT "FP32" engine is not off the Tensor Cores: TF32 is on, which is why the FP32 engines cost only +0.02 .. +1.05 ms per call.

## Gates (unchanged from the earlier briefs)

xseg IoU >= 0.995 on >= 99% of faces and none < 0.95; landmark p95 <= 0.3 frame px.

## Reading

- **The error is diffuse, not in the ops the brief names.** Keeping every normalisation statistic, Sigmoid and Gemm in FP32 changes xseg IoU by 0.0002 and landmark p95 by < 0.01 px. The 3.2 px on 1k3d68 is the same order as the 10-10 TRT-`mixed` measurement (p95 4.5 px), i.e. FP16 in the conv stack itself.
- **Same failure as TRT mixed, now confirmed by an independent route** (ONNX-level conversion rather than a TRT builder flag): xseg min IoU 0.81 vs 0.81, 1k3d68 refined kps ~9-10% IOD. `refine_landmarks` writes those kps into `face.kps`, so the error reaches the TPS/crop alignment.
- **Slower, not faster, where it matters:** xseg +9% and 1k3d68 +34% on the CUDA EP (cast nodes + NHWC transposes; the CUDA EP's FP16 kernels do not beat its FP32 ones at batch 1 on these small graphs). Only 2d106det gains (-0.18 ms) and it fails the gate by 2.6x.
- **Ceiling on the upside:** the shipped FP32 engines cost +0.6 / +0.02 / +1.05 ms per call over mixed. The pipeline is GPU-bound and end-to-end changes of ~1% are below the rig's resolution (AGENTS.md).

## Decisions

- `PRECISION_OVERRIDES` unchanged. No new "aux precision" setting: `ROOP_TRT_MODEL_PRECISION="xseg:mixed;..."` already is the override, and a compute-capability >= 8.0 default of FP16 would be the configuration that fails these gates on both the 3060 and 4070.
- RF++ and GPEN: both already run TRT `mixed` (FP16) on the 4070; on the 3060 TRT is not admitted so there is no FP16 engine to select. Their fidelity is a separate, already-recorded finding (`restorer_fp16_islands_2026-10-10.md`).
- "Tensor Core utilization indicators" in `benchmark_pipeline.py`: none exist, and the harness cannot see them (needs Nsight / DCGM `tensor_active`). Not invented here.

## Not measured

- **The RTX 3060.** All numbers are the 4070. The CUDA-EP speed result may differ on Ampere mobile; the *fidelity* result is hardware-independent enough that no speed gain would rescue it.
- End-to-end fps (nothing was wired in).

app/env/Scripts/python.exe tests/trt_precision_fidelity.py capture --faces 500    # once, ~3 min
